# Calculator
A High perfomance , low level programming Calculator built for systems developers , embedded engineers , and hardware hackers , well it can be by anyone who wants to . This tool combines a sleek desktop workstation layout with binary , hexadecimal , and ASCII inspection capabilities .

## Features
* **Real-time Multi-Base Conversion:** Instantaneous translation across hexadecimal(HEX) , decimal(DEC) , Octal(OCT) , and Binary (BIN) . Includes horizontol scrolling big numbers.
* **Interactive 32 bit Register:** A live visual grid displaying individual bit states . Click any bit to instantly toggle its value ('0' or '1').
* **Byte & ASCII Inspector Panel:** Instantly breaks down 32 Bit Integer into its 4 individual component bytes  , showing each byte's hex value, decimal value and its live printable ASCII character translation .
* **Persistent Calculation history:** Automatically Saves your Calculations to browser `local storage`.Click any previous entry to reload its value and display it into the display , or clear it with a single click .
* **Custom Themes:** Switch asthetics instantly with built in themes :
 * **Default Dark:** Sleek, modern Programmer Dark mode .
 * **Matrix Green:** High-contrast Terminal Green.
 * **Synthwave:** Vibrant neon Cyberpunk feeling.
* **Keyboard Support:** Fully Operable via keyboard shortcuts for lightning-fast Calculations:
 * Hex Digits (`0-9`,`A-F`)
 * Bitwise Operations(`&`,`|`,`^` mapped to exponentiation/power, and standard arithematic operators )
 * `Enter` / `=` to calculate , `Backspace` to delete , `esc` to clear.
